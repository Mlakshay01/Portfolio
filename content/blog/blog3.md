## The Gap

In 2025, Tesla delivered about **1.64 million** vehicles, its second consecutive annual decline. BYD sold about **2.26 million** fully electric passenger cars, up nearly 28%.

2026 is more complicated. Through September, Tesla delivered **1.32 million** (+8.8%). BYD sold **1.63 million** (+1.5%).

Tesla is growing faster, but BYD is still **305,000 cars ahead**, and sold 56.7% more than Tesla in Q3 alone.

## Why BYD Is Winning Today

**Range.** BYD sells everything from city cars to family SUVs, in both fully electric and plug-in hybrid form. Tesla sells a handful of premium-leaning models.

**Control.** BYD makes its own batteries, including its LFP Blade Battery, so it controls cost, supply and design in one system. China also dominates global battery manufacturing, per the [IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/manufacturing-and-trade).

**Exports.** In September, BYD's overseas shipments hit ~179,877, up 153.9% year over year ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/chinas-byd-sales-rise-september-export-boom-sustains-momentum-2026-10-01/)). *(Total NEV figures, including hybrids.)*

## What Tesla Is Betting On

Tesla is moving Full Self-Driving to a [subscription-only model](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/tesla-offer-self-driving-software-only-monthly-basis-feb-14-musk-says-2026-01-14/) in the US. Recurring software revenue and robotaxis could be worth far more than car sales.

But Tesla itself calls the product [FSD (Supervised)](https://www.tesla.com/support/full-self-driving-subscriptions): drivers must stay attentive. Supervised driver assistance is not a robotaxi network.

## The Real Risks

- **BYD:** can scale become profit, given price wars, tariffs and the cost of entering new markets?
- **Tesla:** can autonomy become a real business before rivals take more of today's market?

Sales volume alone doesn't show profitability, so watch margins and cash flow too.

## The Takeaway

Being first doesn't guarantee staying first. Tesla is building for the future; BYD is aggressively winning the present. The test is whether Tesla can do both, and whether BYD can turn momentum into lasting global leadership.
